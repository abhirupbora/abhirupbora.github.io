DOT Matrix Gaming Console

An open-source retro gaming system built meticulously from scratch. Utilizing an 8x8 LED matrix display driven by an ESP32 microcontroller, this project merges vintage aesthetic with modern wireless connectivity. It features seamless Bluetooth gamepad integration, allowing for lag-free retro gaming built purely on custom C++ logic.

Watch Project Video Documentation on GitHub

Casio SA-21 MIDI Conversion

A micro-electronics reverse engineering project that resurrects a vintage Casio SA-21 keyboard. By mapping its original matrix wiring and interfacing it with an ESP32 microcontroller, the keyboard is transformed into a fully functional, zero-latency USB MIDI controller capable of interfacing with modern digital audio workstations.

Watch Project Video Documentation on GitHub

Automated Thermal Power Switch

A hardware automation build focusing on smart environmental control. This system utilizes thermal sensors and microcontrollers to autonomously manage power states based on temperature thresholds, representing a practical application of IoT logic in energy management systems.

Watch Project Video Documentation on GitHub

DIY Guitar Amplifier

An analog electronics project involving the complete design and construction of a custom guitar amplifier. Utilizing discrete components, op-amps, and careful acoustic housing design, this build aims to deliver clean, highly responsive audio amplification tailored for live instrument playback.

The Rise of Omongol

The rain over the hills of the old town never seemed to stop. Seeking refuge from a violent summer thunderstorm, Rye pushed open the heavy oak doors of an abandoned, centuries-old library. The air inside was thick with the scent of petrichor and decaying parchment. In the flickering, melancholic gold of a single lantern, he found her—a striking stranger draped in a simple grey shirt, her eyes holding the depth of a forgotten ocean.

What began as quiet, intellectual banter amidst the dusty shelves rapidly evolved into a breathtaking, heart-fluttering romance. But every soul in this town cast a shadow. Hers was an ancestral curse, a bloodline mandate binding her to a slumbering cosmic entity known only as the Youtun, Omongol.

As the lines between reality and nightmare begin to blur, affection warps into terror. Rye is thrust into a desperate, supernatural struggle where the sky fractures, and he must decide: is a love that defies the stars worth tearing the world apart?

Fate or Faith

Anthony’s entire world was framed by the cold glass of an aquarium and the metal wheels of his chair. A brilliant but deeply introverted writer, he had long accepted that his life was a spectator sport, watching others move while he remained still. Then came Charlotte.

She was a sunbeam bursting through the quiet blue light of the clinic—radiant, unabashedly extroverted, and utterly relentless in her kindness. She didn't just see the boy confined to the chair; she saw the storyteller within. Her laughter became the anchor Anthony never knew he needed.

As the seasons drift by in a breathtaking montage of stolen glances and profound emotional confessions, Charlotte discovers her own quiet obsession. Driven by a love so pure it borders on the miraculous, she dedicates her brilliant mind to science and engineering. She isn't just building mechanical legs for the boy she loves. She is declaring war on fate itself, determined to ensure they can walk side-by-side beneath a sky full of stars.

The Lift Incident

It was supposed to be a mundane Tuesday. Just a normal, forgettable afternoon hanging out with friends. But when the heavy metal doors slid shut and the elevator ground to a violent, echoing halt halfway between the third and fourth floors, the reality of the hanging steel box finally set in.

First came the heavy, awkward silence. Then came the panic. But inevitably, as oxygen seemed to thin and time stretched into an uncomfortable infinity, the claustrophobia birthed something else entirely: uncontrollable, ridiculous laughter.

Stripped of phone signals and stranded in mid-air, the enclosed space became a confession booth for pitch-black humor, absurd arguments, and chaotic camaraderie. This is the hilarious, heartwarming, and entirely true slice-of-life tale of how a mechanical failure forged an unforgettable memory, proving that sometimes the absolute best adventures happen when you're going absolutely nowhere.

The Silent Airport

Bombay Airport at four in the morning is a kingdom of ghosts. It is a vast, liminal space—a gleaming labyrinth of polished floors, humming fluorescent lights, and absolute, deafening silence.

The quiet was almost peaceful, right up until the sickening realization struck: my flight was boarding, and I was stranded on the wrong side of security. With my power bank unceremoniously seized by tired guards, and the departure clock mercilessly ticking down its final minutes, the morning warped into a desperate, adrenaline-fueled sprint.

Lungs burning, bags flying, dodging the few sleepy janitors scattered across the corridors, my heart pounded a frantic, cinematic rhythm against the quiet. This is a story of urban isolation, the sheer terror of missed chances, and an anxiety-inducing race against time across the longest, emptiest terminal in the world.
